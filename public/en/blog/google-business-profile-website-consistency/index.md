# Google Business Profile and website: which details should agree?

German alternate: https://www.vibeperform.com/de/blog/google-unternehmensprofil-website-abgleichen/
Summary: A checklist for business name, contact details, hours and services, with responsibilities and important differences between your profile and website.

Topic: Aligning your business presence
Published: 2026-09-30
Author: Marlon Dietrich
Reading time: 4 min read

A customer discovers your business on Google Maps, then opens the website. If the phone number or opening hours differ, it becomes unclear which information to trust. Aligning the two therefore starts with current facts from your business.

The website and business profile serve different purposes. Their information should describe the same business, but they do not need to display every field identically. In particular, a business address is not automatically a location open to customers.

## 1. Establish the right starting point

First check whether a suitable profile already exists, who owns it and who is authorised to edit it. If access is missing, do not rush to create another profile. Also record who can update the website and who in the business confirms its information.

Eligibility for a Business Profile and its correct presentation depend on the actual business activity. Consult [Google’s guidelines for representing your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177). Having a website alone does not establish eligibility.

## 2. Compare essential details side by side

| Detail | What to compare |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Name actually used on the profile, website and business materials; no extra search terms added to the name |
| Phone and contact | Current number managed by the business and functioning contact routes |
| Website link | Correct website or relevant location page; open and test it on a smartphone |
| Opening hours | Accurate hours, special hours and a clear distinction between visits and availability |
| Location and service area | Actual business arrangements; no invented branches or fictitious addresses |
| Services | Work currently offered, clear descriptions and consistent boundaries |
| Images | Current, usable photos of your own business or work; check rights and permissions |

This table is a working list. Not every detail belongs everywhere. “Visits by appointment” should be explained clearly on the website so customers do not expect a shop that is always open.

## 3. Handle location and service area correctly

A business receiving customers at its premises has a different starting point from a tradesperson working exclusively at customers’ locations. Google requires businesses without customer visits at their location to hide the address in their profile. Do not use artificial locations to appear in additional towns. Check the specific presentation against the linked guidelines before changing location details.

A website can explain a genuine service area without presenting a supposed business base in every town. Legally required website address information needs separate review. Alignment does not mean publishing an address hidden in the profile everywhere else by default.

## 4. Prepare and review changes together

**Illustrative example, not a client reference:** A trade business changes its telephone availability and now receives visitors only by appointment. The owner confirms the new information. The responsible person prepares the website change and appropriate profile fields. After approval, both are updated and the visible details are checked. Whether a particular change is possible as a profile field is checked in the existing profile.

For each comparison, keep a short list: field, previous detail, confirmed new detail, responsible person and implementation status. Google may review changes or require further confirmations. Submitting a change does not establish that it is already publicly visible.

## 5. Agree upkeep and expectations

Decide how new services, moves and changed hours are reported. Changes prompted by business events need a clear owner; extra posts and review responses should have their own agreed scope and approval route.

Complete, current information helps people find their way. It does not establish a fixed local ranking. Google names relevance, distance and prominence among the factors in its [explanation of local rankings](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).
