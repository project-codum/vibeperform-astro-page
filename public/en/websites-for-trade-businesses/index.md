## Before your decision.

### How much does a website cost?

Websites usually start at €1,800 including VAT. The price depends on how many additional pages are needed.

### Can we keep our existing website?

We review the system, hosting and available access. We then clarify which content and features can be reused and whether targeted changes or a rebuild make sense.

### Do we need finished copy and photos?

No. We need your knowledge of the business and existing material. We develop the copy and select suitable images, agreeing any additional photos or graphics with you beforehand.

### Is a careers page included?

If recruitment is your goal, we plan suitable content and an application journey. The specific scope is set out in your proposal.

### What happens after I enquire?

We review your answers and your website, if you have one. You receive a personal assessment with three practical improvements. The review is free and does not commit you to a website project.

### Do you guarantee enquiries or applications?

We cannot promise a fixed number. We create the foundations: clear services, credible insights into your business and an easy contact journey. Your offering, demand and location affect the outcome.
